# Vehicle Borrow Kiosk — User Guide

Welcome! This kiosk lets you borrow a vehicle and return it when you're done.

---

## Starting a Borrow

1. On the home screen, tap **Borrow a Car**.
2. You'll see all the vehicles. Each one shows its current status:
   - **✅ Available** — free to take
   - **🚗 Currently out** — someone else has it
   - **📅 Reserved today** — someone has booked it for today
   - **⚠️ Overdue** — the last borrower is late
3. Tap an **Available** car.
4. Enter:
   - **Your name**
   - **Where you're going** (destination)
5. Tap **Confirm Borrow**.

You're done. The car is now yours until you bring it back.

**There's no return time.** Bring the car back whenever your task is complete.

---

## Returning a Vehicle

1. On the home screen, tap **Return a Car**.
2. Tap the car you're bringing back.
3. Confirm your name on the next screen.
4. Fill in:
   - **Fuel level** (0–100, as a percentage)
   - **Current mileage**
   - **Notes** — anything you noticed, e.g. "wipers aren't working"
5. Tap **Confirm Return**.

The car is now available for the next person.

**Please fill in notes if anything is wrong with the car.** It helps the team fix issues quickly.

---

## Reserving a Vehicle in Advance

If you need a specific vehicle for a future date range, reserve it instead of waiting until the day.

1. On the home screen, tap **Reserve for Later**.
2. Tap the car you want.
3. Fill in:
   - **Your name**
   - **Destination**
   - **Start date** — the first day you need it
   - **End date** — the day you plan to bring it back
   - **Notes** (optional)
4. Tap **Confirm Reservation**.

### Your Pickup Code

After confirming, you'll see a **4-character code** like **A7K2**.

> **Write this code down.**
> You'll need it to pick up the car. It won't be shown again.

If you lose the code, contact the admin.

---

## Picking Up a Reserved Vehicle

On the day of your reservation:

1. On the home screen, tap **Borrow a Car**.
2. Find your reserved car — it will show **📅 Reserved today by [your name]**.
3. Tap it.
4. You'll see **"Is this you?"** with your name.
5. Enter your **pickup code**.
6. Tap **Confirm Pickup**.

The car is now yours for your reservation period.

---

## Returning Early

If you finish early and bring the car back **before your reservation ends**, the system will ask:

> **Do you still want to keep it reserved?**

You have two choices:

- **Yes, keep it** — the car stays reserved for you. You can pick it up again later within your reservation period.
- **No, cancel reservation** — you're done. The reservation is released, and the car becomes available for anyone.

Choose based on whether you plan to use the car again before your reservation ends.

---

## Rules

- **A reserved car is locked to the reserver.** On a reserved day, only the person with the pickup code can take it.
- **Return the car when you're done.** There's no time limit on walk-up borrows, but don't hold onto it longer than necessary.
- **If a reservation isn't picked up**, it expires naturally at the end of its date range. Nothing to do.
- **Notes matter.** If something is broken or off, write it down when you return the car.

---

## Troubleshooting

### "This car is currently out"

Someone else has it. Pick a different car, or try again later.

### "This car is overdue"

The previous borrower hasn't returned it. Contact the admin — you can't take this car right now.

### "That code does not match this reservation"

Double-check your pickup code. It's the 4-character code you got when you made the reservation. Codes are case-insensitive — `A7K2` and `a7k2` both work.

### I lost my pickup code

Contact the admin. They can look it up for you.

### The screen is stuck / didn't respond

Refresh the page. If it's still stuck, contact the admin.
